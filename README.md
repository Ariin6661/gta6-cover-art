Vice Coast Cover Studio, an offline web app that turns your photos into GTA 6-style cover art (multi-panel posters, bold lettering, Vice City sunset/neon grades, PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080).

## What it does

- Four cover layouts: Poster (hero photo over three panels), Nine Panel (eight photos around a title block), Full Bleed (one photo with a title band), and Tilted Snaps (three rotated polaroids on a sunset sky).
- Five colour grades: Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Vice Violet, and Noir.
- Load up to four of your own photos, pick one per panel, drag to pan and scroll to zoom each photo, and edit the title, subtitle, and badge.
- Exports a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, and 1920×1080. Everything runs offline in the browser — your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
